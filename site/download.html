Get Moly

Android

Get it on Google Play Download the APK

Works on its own, no computer app needed. Also on itch.io, as a signed APK on the releases page, and through Obtainium.

Browser extensions

Chrome Edge Firefox

The extensions need the small Moly app on your computer (below). Install the extension, then press "Check again" in Moly.

The computer app runs on your computer and starts with your browser. Linux is the tested system; the Windows and Mac installers are experimental.

How to get a key

Moly writes with Claude or OpenAI using a key that belongs to you (a ChatGPT or Claude chat subscription does not include one), or with a model on your own computer (Ollama, computers only). Create an account, add a small credit and a spending limit, and make a key: Claude keys or OpenAI keys. It has been tested with Claude; OpenAI support is experimental. The key stays encrypted on your device.

Linux

Download the .deb package
Debian, Ubuntu, Mint and relatives (x86-64). Open it with your software installer, or run sudo apt install ./moly_*.deb. For all users of the computer.

Or open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://nireus79.github.io/Moly/install.sh | sh

For your user only, no root. It checks the download before installing.

Mac (experimental)

Download the installer (.pkg)
Double-click it. The beta is not signed: if macOS will not open it, right-click it and choose Open.

Or open Terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://nireus79.github.io/Moly/install.sh | sh

Never run on a Mac by the developer. The beta is not code-signed; the installer handles that. If macOS still objects, choose "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Windows (experimental)

Download the installer

Tested only under Wine, not on a real Windows machine. Run it. The beta is not signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
