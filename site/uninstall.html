Remove Moly

You removed the Moly extension from your browser. The Moly app is still on your computer: a browser extension cannot remove programs. To remove it too, follow the steps for your system.

Windows

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps.
  2. Find Moly and choose Uninstall.

Linux

If you installed the .deb package, open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

sudo apt remove moly

If you installed with the one-line command, paste this instead:

~/.local/share/moly/uninstall.sh

Mac

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

"$HOME/Library/Application Support/moly-program/uninstall.sh"

If you installed the .pkg, paste this instead:

sudo /usr/local/lib/moly/uninstall.sh

Your conversations

Removing the app keeps your conversations, people and settings in the .moly folder of your user folder, encrypted. If you install Moly again, they are there. To delete them as well, add --purge to the Linux or Mac command above, or delete the .moly folder yourself.

Removed the extension by mistake? Install Moly again; nothing is lost.
