Moly

Better thinking before you hit send. Moly asks the one question that matters, then helps you write. It never decides for you.

How it works

  1. You describe what you want to say, to whom, and why.
  2. Moly asks one question about what is missing (who the person is, what you want).
  3. When it knows enough, it helps you write the message. You can skip the questions at any time.

Private by design

  • Your conversations are stored on your computer, in an encrypted database.
  • Use a model on your own computer (Ollama), or your own Claude or OpenAI key (OpenAI support is experimental in this beta), protected by a passphrase.
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising network. Free and open source (MIT). A small card with a message from Moly or a sponsor may appear in its own area below the writing box, never between messages; you can hide it until you open Moly again.

Install

Moly has two parts: the extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox, and a small app on your computer that starts and stops with your browser.

Download the app

Instructions for Windows, Mac and Linux: INSTALL.md. If the extension says "Install the Moly app", run the installer and press "Check again".

Good to know

  • Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service.
  • It is a beta. Questions and problems: Hermes_creative@proton.me, or GitHub issues.
  • Read the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and what leaves your computer.
Moly (Μώλυ) is named after the herb in Homer's Odyssey that protects Odysseus from Circe's spell. Published by Hermes Software.
