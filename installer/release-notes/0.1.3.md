**Moly 0.1.3.** The desktop app, the browser extension and the Android app carry the same version number.

**What is new for the computer**
- **The background app closes faster.** About ten seconds after the last browser is closed (it was thirty), and never while a reply is still being written.
- **OpenAI keys:** replies now use OpenAI's small "mini" model, which is faster and costs far less on your own key than the flagship. Claude is unchanged.
- **Your language.** Write to Moly in any language and it answers in that language. The buttons, settings and manual are English only for now.
- **The sponsor card** has its new layouts, and the card can still be changed from the website without a new version. Every card is labelled "Sponsored".
- The installer self-test now takes the expected version from the version file, so it cannot be left behind by a release.

**Install:** Windows: download and run `moly-setup-0.1.3.exe`. Linux: open `moly_0.1.3_amd64.deb` with your software installer, or run `curl -fsSL https://nireus79.github.io/Moly/install.sh | sh`. Mac: open `moly-0.1.3-macos-universal.pkg`. Then install the extension from your browser's store and press "Check again". Step by step: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Nireus79/Moly/blob/master/INSTALL.md). Moly for Android is on Google Play, and its APK is below.

**Not signed.** The installers are not code-signed yet: Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (choose More info, then Run anyway); on a Mac right-click the installer and choose Open.

**Not tried:** the Mac `.pkg` on a real Mac, OpenAI with a real key (experimental), and the interface in languages other than English.

Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
