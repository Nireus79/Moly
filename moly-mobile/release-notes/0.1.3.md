**Moly 0.1.3 for Android.**

**What is new since 0.1.2**
- **The engine stops within seconds when you close Moly** (Back, or swipe it away). Before, it could stay up for minutes. Replies being written are finished first.
- **OpenAI keys:** replies now use OpenAI's small model, which is faster and costs far less than its flagship. Claude is unchanged.
- **Better problem reports:** Settings, Report a problem now shows the last crash of Moly's engine (kind and code locations only, never your messages).
- **The new sponsor card** (the same short, labelled card as on the computer; it never reads what you write).
- **Language:** write to Moly in any language and it answers in that language. Its buttons and settings are English only for now.

**Install:** download `moly-0.1.3.apk` below and open it. It updates an earlier version in place and keeps your data (same signing key). Check the file against `SHA256SUMS` if you like.

**You need** an Android 7 or newer phone (64-bit) and your own Claude or OpenAI key. Moly has no server: your messages go from the phone to the provider you chose, with your key, and nowhere else.

Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
