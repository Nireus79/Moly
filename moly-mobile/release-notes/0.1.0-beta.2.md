Second beta of **Moly for Android**. One change since beta.1: you can now report a reply.

**What is new**
- Settings, *Report a problem or a reply*: write or paste your own note (for example a reply from Moly that you want to report) above the technical report. You read everything, tap *Send report*, and your mail app opens addressed to the support address with the text filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. Phones with no mail app get the share sheet instead.

Everything else is as in beta.1. Moly is the same Socratic writing coach as the browser extension, in an app: no computer, no account, no ads. You need an Android 7 or newer phone (64-bit) and your own Claude or OpenAI key. Moly has no server: your messages go from the phone to the provider you chose, with your key, and nowhere else.

**Install:** download `moly-0.1.0-beta.2.apk` below on your phone and open it. If beta.1 is installed, it updates in place and keeps your data (same signing key). Check the file against `SHA256SUMS` if you like. Step by step: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Nireus79/Moly/blob/master/INSTALL.md#android-beta).

**Tried so far:** a Xiaomi Mi A2 (Android 10) with Claude, and emulators with Android 8.1 and 14. **Not tried:** OpenAI with a real key (experimental), 32-bit phones (not included), and days of background use on phones with strict battery managers (if a reply never arrives, allow Moly to run in the background in the phone's battery settings and send a report).

Beta software: expect rough edges. Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
