Third beta of **Moly for Android**. It stops running in the background when you are not using it, and opens on a blank chat after a longer time away.

**What is new since beta.2**
- **Moly stops by itself when idle.** A few minutes after you leave the app (3 to 8, depending on what Moly was doing), its engine stops and the "Moly is running" notice goes away. A reply that is being written is never cut off. Opening Moly starts it again; the first message after that can take a few seconds longer.
- **A fresh start after a longer time away.** If you were away for less than 10 minutes, Moly shows the conversation you left. After longer, it opens on a blank chat; the old conversation stays in the history. A reply being written, or text in the writing box, is never thrown away.

Everything else is as in beta.2. If beta.2 or beta.1 is installed, this updates it in place and keeps your data (same signing key).

**Install:** download `moly-0.1.0-beta.3.apk` below on your phone and open it. Check the file against `SHA256SUMS` if you like. Step by step: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Nireus79/Moly/blob/master/INSTALL.md#android-beta).

**You need** an Android 7 or newer phone (64-bit) and your own Claude or OpenAI key. Moly has no server: your messages go from the phone to the provider you chose, with your key, and nowhere else.

**Tried so far:** a Xiaomi Mi A2 (Android 10) with Claude, and emulators with Android 8.1 and 14. **Not tried:** OpenAI with a real key (experimental), 32-bit phones (not included), and days of background use on phones with strict battery managers (if a reply never arrives, allow Moly to run in the background in the phone's battery settings and send a report from Settings).

Beta software: expect rough edges. Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
