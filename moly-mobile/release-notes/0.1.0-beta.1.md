First beta of **Moly for Android**: the same Socratic writing coach as the browser extension, in an app. No computer, no account, no ads.

**You need** an Android 7 or newer phone (64-bit) and your own Claude or OpenAI key. Moly has no server: your messages go from the phone to the provider you chose, with your key, and nowhere else.

**Install:** download `moly-0.1.0-beta.1.apk` below on your phone and open it (Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` if you like. Step by step: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Nireus79/Moly/blob/master/INSTALL.md#android-beta).

**What works**
- Setup with a pasted key, chat, a reply that finishes while you are in another app, share text to Moly from any app, light and dark mode.
- Your data stays on the phone in an encrypted database; Android backup is off. Settings, *Save a copy* and *Restore from a copy* move it to a file you choose (uninstalling deletes your conversations).
- Settings, *Report a problem* prepares a short note about the phone and Moly's engine. You read it, and it goes nowhere unless you share it.

**Tried so far:** a Xiaomi Mi A2 (Android 10) with Claude, and emulators with Android 8.1 and 14. **Not tried:** OpenAI with a real key (it is marked experimental), 32-bit phones (not included in this APK), and days of background use on phones with strict battery managers (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung: if a reply never arrives, allow Moly to run in the background in the phone's battery settings and send us a report).

Beta software: expect rough edges. Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
