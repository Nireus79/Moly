Fourth beta of **Moly for Android**. The app is now built for Android 16 (the target Google Play requires), with no change in what it does.

**What is new since beta.3**
- **Built for Android 16.** The app targets Android 16 (API 36) and still runs on Android 7 and newer. It was tested on Android 8.1, 14 and 16 (emulators) and on a real Android 10 phone.
- **Ready for phones with 16 KB memory pages.** The engine inside the app is built for them.
- **Android's limit on background services.** Android 15 and later allow this kind of background service only about six hours a day; Moly now stops its engine cleanly when Android asks.
- **Build.** The app builds with the free Debian tools in F-Droid's build image (JDK 21, Go 1.26, Node 22); the recipe is in `moly-mobile/fdroid`.

Everything else is as in beta.3 (stops when idle, opens blank after a longer time away, report a reply). If an earlier beta is installed, this updates it in place and keeps your data (same signing key).

**Install:** download `moly-0.1.0-beta.4.apk` below on your phone and open it. Check the file against `SHA256SUMS` if you like. Step by step: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Nireus79/Moly/blob/master/INSTALL.md#android-beta).

**You need** an Android 7 or newer phone (64-bit) and your own Claude or OpenAI key. Moly has no server: your messages go from the phone to the provider you chose, with your key, and nowhere else.

**Not tried:** OpenAI with a real key (experimental), 32-bit phones (not included), and days of background use on phones with strict battery managers (if a reply never arrives, allow Moly to run in the background in the phone's battery settings and send a report from Settings).

Beta software: expect rough edges. Moly is a writing coach, not a crisis, medical or legal service. Problems and questions: Hermes_creative@proton.me or the issues page.
